# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes are applied to the current production code on `main`. Historical
commits, development branches, and local copies are not separately supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue, discussion,
or pull request. Submit it privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/bobbynecker/city-of-sealy/security/advisories/new).

If the private form is unavailable, contact the repository owner using the
business contact listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Share only enough
information to establish a private channel; do not send credentials, personal
information, municipal records, or exploit details through a public channel.

Include, when available:

- the affected URL, component, commit, or version;
- the security impact and who could be affected;
- reproducible steps using test data only;
- a minimal proof of concept, with secrets and personal data removed; and
- any suggested mitigation.

We aim to acknowledge complete reports within three business days and provide
an initial triage decision within seven business days. Remediation timing
depends on severity, complexity, and the need to coordinate a safe release.

## Safe-harbor boundaries

Use only accounts and data you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Do not
access, retain, alter, or disclose another person's data; degrade service;
perform denial-of-service or social-engineering tests; or test third-party
systems outside this repository's control. Stop testing and report immediately
if you encounter sensitive information.
